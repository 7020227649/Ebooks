```markdown
We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

For more information on how Google processes data when you use our site, please review Google's Privacy & Terms at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

```

## 3. High-Trust "About Us" Framework


To pass manual E-E-A-T review, structure your `/about` page with these four sections:



1. **Our Mission**: A clear, 2-paragraph statement of what specific problem your site solves for readers.<br />2. **Who We Are**: Introduce the founder or primary contributors by name, accompanied by a genuine photo or avatar.<br />3. **Our Editorial Standards**: Explicitly describe how content is researched, fact-checked, and updated. Mention that articles are written or verified by knowledgeable practitioners.<br />4. **Contact & Social Presence**: Provide a direct email address (`contact@yourdomain.com`) and links to your verified professional profiles (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, GitHub).
