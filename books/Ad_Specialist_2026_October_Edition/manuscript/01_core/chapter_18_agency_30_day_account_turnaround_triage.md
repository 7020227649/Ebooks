# Chapter 18: The 30-Day Agency Account Turnaround & Emergency Triage Blueprint

## 1. The Emergency Room Mandate: Inheriting a Failing Account

When an enterprise performance agency takes over a client spending $100k/month at a bleeding 1.1 ROAS, you do not have 6 months to run exploratory branding studies. **You have exactly 30 days to stop the bleed, restore profitability, and prove competence.**

## 2. Week 1 (Days 1–7): Triage Audit & Waste Bleed Purge

### Step 1: Kill the "Zombie Ads"
* Pull a 60-day account export. Filter for ad sets and ads with spend $> 3\times \text{ Target CPA}$ and zero conversions. Pause immediately.
* **Result**: Recovers $20\% - 35\%$ of wasted daily budget instantly.

### Step 2: Purge Mobile App & MFA Placements
* In Google Ads and PMax, inject account-level negative exclusions blocking all 140+ mobile app categories.
* Block low-tier YouTube channels and clickbait display inventory.

### Step 3: Stop Brand Cannibalization
* Add the client's own brand name, domain, and common misspellings to account-level Negative Keyword Lists in generic Search and PMax campaigns.
* Isolate brand search into a dedicated single-budget campaign running Target Impression Share ($95\%$ absolute top).

---

## 3. Week 2 (Days 8–14): Creative Overhaul & Visual Framework Injection

Failing accounts almost always suffer from creative exhaustion—running 1 stale video from 6 months ago.
* Deploy **10 fresh static and video assets** based on the 8 proven frameworks from Chapter 7:
  - 2x *"Us vs. Them"* Comparison Statics
  - 2x Anatomic Feature Callout Graphics
  - 2x Verified Trustpilot Review Overlays
  - 2x UGC Problem-Agony Hook Videos
  - 2x Native Notes App / Tweet Thought Leadership Statics
* Launch into an isolated **3:2:2 Dynamic Creative Testing Sandbox**.

---

## 4. Week 3 (Days 15–21): Signal Consolidation & CAPI Calibration

* **Deploy Serverless Meta CAPI Gateway**: Replace broken client-side pixel with direct Node.js/Cloudflare CAPI dispatcher, pushing EMQ from 4.8 to $\ge 9.2$.
* **Merge Fragmented Ad Sets**: Collapse 25 micro-targeted interest ad sets into **1 Broad Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC)**.
* **Signal Density Target**: Ensure the consolidated campaign captures $\ge 50$ conversions within 7 days, exiting Learning Limited permanently.

---

## 5. Week 4 (Days 22–30): Scale Validation & Executive WPR

* Verify that blended ROAS and Contribution Margin have stabilized above breakeven threshold.
* Initiate **+15% 48-hour vertical budget scaling** on verified winning creatives.
* Present the **Executive Turnaround Scorecard** to client leadership, demonstrating CAC reduction and EBITDA recovery.
